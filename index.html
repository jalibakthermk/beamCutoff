File and method

  • Regulation basis: UN R149, 00 series (original version) as amended by its supplements. Photometric values from Table 9 (Class C), Imax segment and cut-off position from Table 10, Zone III from Table 11, cut-off quality and instrumental aiming from Annex 6. Verify limits against your controlled copy of the 00 series, Supplement 6 before using the result for approval work.
  • Angles: Type A/B files are read as screen angles (H right, V up). Type C files are mapped H = C-plane angle, V = γ − 90°. Use the axis options if the file uses another sign convention.
  • Gradient G = log I(β) − log I(β + 0.1°), evaluated on a 0.01° resampled scan of the bilinear-interpolated file. The cut-off is the inflection point (maximum G). Only transitions with a bright zone below are counted.
  • Minimum sharpness uses the better of the 10 m/Ø10 mm and 25 m/Ø30 mm detector apertures; maximum sharpness uses 25 m/Ø30 mm, as in Annex 6 §2. Simulation files on grids coarser than 0.05° give a biased G.
  • Horizontal aim. Geometric kink: intersection of the horizontal part with a straight-line fit of the inclined part. Method (a): inflection on a horizontal scan at 0.2°D (after vertical aim) placed on V-V. Method (b): inflections on vertical scans at 1R, 2R, 3R, line through them, intersection with the cut-off line B placed on V-V. Methods (a) and (b) are what the applicant declares for instrumental aiming; for a 15° cut-off they put the geometric kink left of V-V.
  • One lamp is evaluated with its full intensity. Footnote minima "each side ≥ 50 cd" are applied to this lamp. S50/S100 sums are evaluated without position lamps. LHT beams are mirrored and checked with L and R swapped.
  • Not covered in this version: other passing-beam classes (V, E, W and A/B/D), bending mode (Table 9 Part B), symmetric passing beams (Classes AS–ES), colour and stability tests.
