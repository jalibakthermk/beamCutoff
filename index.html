File and method

  • Regulation basis: UN R149, 00 series (original version) as amended by its supplements. Photometric values for Class C from Table 9, Imax segment and cut-off position from Table 10 and Zone III from Table 11; for the symmetrical Classes CS and DS from Table 35 (with Zones 1 and 2); cut-off quality and instrumental aiming from Annex 6. Verify limits against your controlled copy of the 00 series, Supplement 6 before using the result for approval work.
  • Angles: Type A/B files are read as screen angles (H right, V up). Type C files are mapped H = C-plane angle, V = γ − 90°. Use the axis options if the file uses another sign convention.
  • Gradient G = log I(β) − log I(β + 0.1°), evaluated on a 0.01° resampled vertical scan. For the scans the file is interpolated linearly in log I, so coarse grids (0.1°–0.2°) no longer inflate G; test points and zones still use linear interpolation in I. The cut-off is the inflection point (maximum G). A G value counts only where the darker of its two intensities is at or above the gradient intensity threshold in cd (default 100 cd, 0 = off) and where the beam is bright 0.3° below. Optional noise reduction averages each scan over a small horizontal band. The page reports where the maximum was found, the two intensities, and the spread of G between 2° and 3° from V-V.
  • Minimum sharpness uses the better of the 10 m/Ø10 mm and 25 m/Ø30 mm detector apertures; maximum sharpness uses 25 m/Ø30 mm, as in Annex 6 §2. Simulation files on grids coarser than 0.05° give a biased G.
  • Horizontal aim. Geometric kink: intersection of the horizontal part with a straight-line fit of the inclined part. Method (a): inflection on a horizontal scan at 0.2°D (after vertical aim) placed on V-V. Method (b): inflections on vertical scans at 1R, 2R, 3R, line through them, intersection with the cut-off line B placed on V-V. Methods (a) and (b) are what the applicant declares for instrumental aiming; for a 15° cut-off they put the geometric kink left of V-V.
  • One lamp is evaluated with its full intensity. Footnote minima "each side ≥ 50 cd" (Class C) and the full point-2 minimum (CS/DS) are applied to this lamp. S50/S100 sums are evaluated without position lamps. LHT beams are mirrored and checked with L and R swapped.
  • Classes CS and DS (symmetrical passing beam): the cut-off inflection on V-V is aimed to 0.57°D and the beam keeps its horizontal position from the file. Cut-off requirements from Annex 6 for symmetrical beams: one cut-off, G ≥ 0.08 at 2.5°L and 2.5°R, cut-off between 3°L and 3°R within ±0.3° of the aimed line, inflections at 3°L and 3°R within 0.3° of nominal. The minimum sums for points 8+9+10 (150 cd) and 11+12+13 (300 cd) and the 0.25° per-point tolerance are not legible in the published 00-series text and were taken from the later R149 text; check them against your copy.
  • Not covered in this version: other passing-beam classes (V, E, W, A/B/D, AS, BS, ES), bending mode (Table 9 Part B), colour and stability tests.
